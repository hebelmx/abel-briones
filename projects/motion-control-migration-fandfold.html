8-Axis Motion Control Migration — Surgical-Drape Folding Machine

Motion Control Allen-Bradley Controls Migration Medical / Semi-Pharma

A surgical operating-room drape folding machine was running on a discontinued Allen-Bradley SLC 500 platform with obsolete 1394 motion drives — a serious unplanned-downtime risk once spare parts ran out. I migrated the full 8-axis motion system to ControlLogix and Kinetix 6500 in a 14-day window, without giving up a single sheet per minute of production rate.

Scope of Work

  • Migrated the SLC 500 PLC to a ControlLogix 1756-L71.
  • Migrated the 10" PanelView 1000 HMI.
  • Migrated 13 servo drives (1394 family) to Kinetix 6500.
  • Migrated 13 servomotors (1326 family) plus 3 AC induction motor drives.
  • Designed, fabricated and installed a new control panel, shipped from our shop to the plant.
  • Commissioned within 14 days with zero reduction in production capability.

Motion Architecture

Eight coordinated axes — Unwinder, Folders/AuxPull, Main Pull (master axis), Cross-Cutter (running a non-linear electronic-cam profile), Separator, Diverter, Crossfolder 1 & 2, PlateFolders, Stackers and Collector — all synchronized off the Main Pull master axis, running up to 40 sheets per minute.

Technology

  • Allen-Bradley ControlLogix 1756-L71, Kinetix 6500, PowerFlex 525
  • 1326 AB servomotors over EtherNet/IP with CIP-Motion
  • Remote I/O (FlexIO) on a star/ring network topology

Deliverables

  • Full electrical and mechanical diagrams
  • Operating manual and troubleshooting guide
