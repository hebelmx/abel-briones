Control System Migration for a Truck Steering Torque Tester

Motion Control Allen-Bradley Controls Migration Automotive

Migrated a legacy PLC5-based test rig — used to apply and measure steering torque on truck steering assemblies — to a modern ControlLogix and Kinetix 6500 platform, with a complete reprogramming of the control logic and a 4-day commissioning window.

The Application

  • The machine applies torque to a truck steering assembly through a servo motor and speed reducer connected to the steering input under test.
  • A hydraulic system of pumps and proportional valves circulates oil through the steering assembly under controlled conditions.
  • A load cell measures the applied torque in real time; the PLC evaluates the readings against configured min/max thresholds together with hydraulic flow values to approve or reject each part.

Scope of Work

  • Removed obsolete control, mechanical and electrical equipment; installed and wired the new platform.
  • Migrated the control platform from Allen-Bradley PLC5 to ControlLogix, and drives from Allen-Bradley 1394 to Kinetix 6500.
  • Complete reprogramming of the PLC control logic — not a like-for-like port.
  • Commissioning and post-commissioning on-site support.

Technical Highlights

  • 3-servo torque control, using Motion Control instruction libraries on the new platform.
  • Closed-loop hydraulic control of pumps and proportional valves synchronized with the torque test sequence.
  • Telesis part-marking control integrated into the test cycle.
  • Barcode reader and encoder configuration and programming for part identification and position feedback.
