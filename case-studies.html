Turnkey Controls Modernization & Run-at-Rate Delivery

Delivered in Production

Context & Stakes

A high-volume manufacturing line needed a production-critical upgrade to legacy PLCs, motion and drives. Once cutover began, the legacy architecture could no longer serve as a fallback.

Role & Decision Authority

I owned the commercial and technical delivery of Exxerpro’s contracted automation workstream, from quotation and controls architecture through factory acceptance, site acceptance, commissioning and run-at-rate. I coordinated controls engineers, panel builders and plant stakeholders within a broader multimillion-dollar installation.

Architectural & Engineering Analysis

I organized the migration around a factory-validated hardware and software baseline, a phased commissioning window and explicit motion and safety checks.

Execution & Verification

The team tested the controls baseline at factory acceptance (FAT), checked interfaces on-site at site acceptance (SAT), and verified motion and safety during live commissioning before the run-at-rate test.

Measured Outcome & Status

The modernized controls workstream reached production run-at-rate acceptance.

Leadership & Scaling Takeaway

Define acceptance criteria before the shutdown. Use the factory test to resolve controls issues while there is still time to act on the findings.

Multi-site OT standards · Mecalux and GM

Engineering Standardization & Technical Escalation

Delivered in Production

Context & Stakes

Lifter installations needed repeatable drive controls and diagnostics aligned with OEM baselines. Automotive integration work also had to satisfy General Motors controls requirements.

Role & Decision Authority

I architected a reusable PowerFlex lifter-controls standard for Mecalux, advised on GM controls compliance and led technical escalations for severe integration and commissioning issues.

Architectural & Engineering Analysis

The standard organized drive control into reusable modules and documented interface contracts, fault diagnostics and commissioning checks.

Execution & Verification

The team used the standard in production delivery. For escalations, I used interface requirements and observed equipment behavior to decide which issues needed engineering intervention during commissioning.

Measured Outcome & Status

The Mecalux PowerFlex standard and GM controls advisory were delivered in production.

Leadership & Scaling Takeaway

Standardize interfaces and diagnostic behavior so commissioning teams have a common baseline when equipment and sites vary.

Smart manufacturing · IndFusion and plant SQL

Deterministic OT-to-Enterprise Data Pipeline

Piloted in Production

Context & Stakes

Fragmented shop-floor signals make it difficult to trust cycle counts and build operational analytics across equipment vendors.

Role & Decision Authority

I directed the architecture and product development of IndFusion, a platform for connecting heterogeneous shop-floor controllers to on-premises and cloud SQL databases.

Architectural & Engineering Analysis

The design separates edge data acquisition from deterministic machine control. It addresses Siemens and Rockwell environments and PROFINET, EtherNet/IP and Modbus TCP integration. The SQL data model supports cycle counts, overall equipment effectiveness (OEE) and energy monitoring.

Execution & Verification

At Mabe, the production pilot integrated Siemens S7-1200 cycle counts with the plant’s SQL Server. This exercised the controller-to-database path with live production data.

Measured Outcome & Status

The Mabe pilot validated cycle counting and integration with plant SQL Server. Predictive analytics remains on the roadmap.

Leadership & Scaling Takeaway

Validate the signal and its meaning at the production source before using it to drive plant-level analytics.

Executive risk governance · ISO 9001 and ExxerCube.Prisma

Engineering Governance, Quality Systems & Auditable AI

ISO 9001 QMS · Delivered in Production ExxerCube.Prisma · In Development (Prototype)

Context & Stakes

Industrial engineering changes and regulatory reviews need evidence that a responsible person can inspect before accepting a result.

Role & Decision Authority

I sponsored and led Exxerpro’s ISO 9001:2015 quality management system and architected ExxerCube.Prisma, an AI-assisted regulatory compliance workflow.

Architectural & Engineering Analysis

The QMS formalized risk assessment, structured diagnostics, verification and validation, and lessons learned. Prisma applies the same review discipline to language-model and retrieval-augmented generation workflows through schema validation, source traceability and required human review.

Execution & Verification

Engineering teams applied the QMS gates to delivery. The Prisma prototype checks structured output against a schema, retains its source evidence and routes the result to a reviewer.

Measured Outcome & Status

Exxerpro’s QMS was delivered in production. ExxerCube.Prisma is in active development as a prototype.

Leadership & Scaling Takeaway

Give each review gate an owner, evidence to inspect and an explicit acceptance decision.
