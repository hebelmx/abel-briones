Engineering Methodology

Team & Task Organization Decision Frameworks Internal Procedure, 2023

The core of this methodology is the root-cause troubleshooting procedure described on the homepage under Engineering Methodology. This page covers its companion piece — a 2023 internal Exxerpro procedure, "Metodología para Organización profesional y laboral" — which applies the same observe-document-analyze-verify discipline to everyday task and team management, mandatory for all staff and collaborators, plus the decision frameworks built into it.

Team & Task Organization

The same discipline, applied beyond troubleshooting

A 12-step process taking a task from first observation through to a documented, reflected-on close — the same rigor as the troubleshooting procedure, applied to day-to-day work and team management.

  1. 1

    Observe

    Gather objective, neutral information about the task before acting.

  2. 2

    Document

    Capture every relevant detail for traceability and future reference; if it's not actionable, file it as reference.

  3. 3

    Organize

    Structure, categorize and store the information and tasks systematically.

  4. 4

    Analyze

    Evaluate implications and effects, positive and negative, before committing.

  5. 5

    Plan

    Set a course of action, sequence the work, and assign it across the team.

  6. 6

    Act

    Execute the plan — with a risk/damage-control check first, even for simple tasks.

  7. 7

    Monitor

    Track progress regularly and correct course as needed.

  8. 8

    Evaluate

    Assess results against objectives.

  9. 9

    Verify

    Confirm quality and compliance with requirements through rigorous review.

  10. 10

    Validate

    Confirm the solution actually resolves the problem without unintended side effects.

  11. 11

    Adjust

    Change scope, timeline or resourcing as the evaluation warrants.

  12. 12

    Reflect

    Capture what worked and what didn't, and document the lesson for next time.

Supporting Frameworks

Decision tools built into the methodology

SMART Objectives

Specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound goal-setting for every task.

Consequence Matrix

Weighs what happens — and what stops happening — if an action is taken versus not taken.

Eisenhower Matrix

Prioritizes work by urgency and importance to decide what to do, delegate, schedule or drop.

SWOT (FODA)

Structured review of strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats before committing to a strategy.

Process Flowcharting

Visual mapping of steps, decisions and flow for any process worth documenting or handing off.
